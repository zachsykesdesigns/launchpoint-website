Happenings

Where the community comes alive

Transformed industrial buildings host regular concerts, food festivals, markets, and gatherings — from Sundays at the M30 Dog Park to festival weekends on the grounds. If it brings people together, it happens here.

Vision

The beating heart of M30 Collective

Monon 30 anchors the wider M30 Collective district — 46 acres along the Monon Trail where people live, gather, and explore. Catch a live show, enjoy local food and drink, or simply soak in the energy: this is where the neighborhood comes together, steps from The Queue townhomes and the Nickel Plate Trail.
