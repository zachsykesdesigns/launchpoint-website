Straight Talk

What you pay for outside our work

Our price covers the work — building, designing, writing, managing. The tools themselves are billed by the companies that make them, in your name, on your accounts. That's deliberate: you own everything, and if we ever part ways, it all stays with you. Here are the typical costs so there are no surprises.

Shopify
~$39/mo

Your online store platform (Growth tier). Paid to Shopify. The platform behind every result on this page.

Square
Free – $49/mo

Register + online store for local shops. If your register is already Square, your store syncs with it.

Wix / Squarespace
~$17–$49/mo

Simple sites and small stores. If you're already on one, we can rebuild right where you live.

WordPress Hosting
~$5–$15/mo

Hosting for a WordPress site (Hostinger, GoDaddy). No platform fee — you own the site outright.

Domain Name
~$15–$20/yr

Your .com, registered in your name. Small cost, non-negotiable to own.

CRM — MarketFlux
Priced by plan

Your customer list, sign-up forms, and automated emails. We set it up; the account is yours.

Meta Ads (Facebook / Instagram)
Your budget, your call

Ad spend is paid by you directly to Meta — never through us, never marked up. We manage it; you control the budget.

Google Workspace
~$7/user/mo

Professional email on your own domain (you@yourbusiness.com) instead of a Gmail address.

Payment Processing
2.9% + 30¢ per sale

The standard card-processing fee, taken out of each sale by Shopify/Stripe/Square. No sale, no fee.

Storage and pick/pack fees paid directly to the warehouse, plus postage. Priced by volume; we help you negotiate it.

Prices are the platforms' typical published rates as of late 2026 and can change — we'll confirm your exact stack and its cost before you sign anything.
