M30 Collective is a 46-acre community along Indianapolis’s Monon Trail where modern living, dining, and recreation come together in one dynamic destination. From residential spaces and vibrant entertainment at MONON 30 to pickleball courts and direct access to the Monon and Nickel Plate Trails, this is urban connectivity reimagined. Whether you’re here to live, gather, or explore, M30 Collective is designed for connection, creativity, and the way life actually happens.

Why It Matters

This isn’t just development.
It’s a rebirth.

Forty-six acres along the Monon Trail are being transformed — a long-overlooked stretch of Indianapolis brought back to life as a place to live, gather, play, and connect. The people around here believe in it, because it’s their neighborhood coming back. That is the story of M30 Collective, and it deserves a presence that rises to it.

The Vision

What We’re Building

We’re creating a neighborhood where everything flows. Imagine living steps from your favorite coffee shop, walking to dinner and a concert, or hitting the trails without getting in your car. At M30 Collective, it’s all connected and designed around real life – a place that works with your rhythm, not against it. This is more than development; it’s a community built to grow and evolve with the people who call it home.

Residential

The Queue

The Queue will offer thoughtfully designed townhomes and apartments within M30 Collective, perfectly positioned along the Monon Trail. This modern residential community features curated amenities and well-crafted indoor and outdoor spaces that balance comfort with connectivity. With immediate access to dining at MONON 30, the Nickel Plate Trail, and nearby green spaces, residents enjoy the best of urban convenience and outdoor living. Offering both market-rate and affordable options, The Queue welcomes a diverse community seeking an accessible, well-connected place to call home.

Entertainment · Dining · Events

M30

M30 is the beating heart of M30 Collective, a dynamic destination for live entertainment, dining, and community events. Housed in transformed industrial buildings, this vibrant venue hosts regular concerts, food festivals, and gatherings that bring people together. Whether you’re catching a live show, enjoying local food and drink, or simply soaking in the energy, MONON 30 is where the community comes alive.

Where It’s Heading

The Domino Falls First.

More than 50 acres of formerly blighted land where the Monon and Nickel Plate trails meet — being rebuilt in sequence, one piece falling into the next:

“The Domino is the first to fall. The Queue is the line to get in. The Turnstile is what you go through to get in.”

The Ground

It started with blight.

Fifty acres of vacant warehouses and empty lots at 30th Street — the ground everything else stands on.

First to Fall · Opened 2025

DOMINO

240 homes, four stories over podium parking, with 800+ feet of frontage on the Monon Trail. The first piece fell — and the neighborhood came back with it.

The Line to Get In · Spring 2027

The Queue

95 townhome-style family rentals — $31 million, now rising on 30th Street. Demolition 2024, vertical construction 2026, doors open Spring 2027.

The District · Spring 2028

M30

17+ acres of warehouse adaptive reuse: hotel, pickleball, food & beverage, recreation and event space — the district the trail runs through.

What You Go Through to Get In · 2026–2029

The Turnstyle

Junction at Fall Creek: 200 mixed-income homes, plus 250 single-family rentals and 250 more multifamily homes — 500+ homes phased through 2029 along the creek and the trails.
